## The short version

This website sets **no cookies**, loads **no trackers**, and runs **no third-party analytics**. There is nothing to consent to, so there is no banner.

## What this site collects

**Nothing, by default.** Pages are static files served from Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare, as hosting provider, processes IP addresses and request metadata to deliver the site and to mitigate abuse; that processing is covered by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). DonIT does not keep server logs of visitors.

**If you use the contact form:** the name, company, email address and message you type are sent to me by email. They are stored nowhere else — not in a database, not in a CRM. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that you are not a bot without setting cookies or tracking you across sites.

**If you email me directly:** your email is processed like any business correspondence.

## Why and for how long

I use contact details only to answer you and, if we work together, to run the engagement. Business correspondence is kept as long as needed for that purpose and for legal (accounting) obligations.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what I hold about you, ask for correction or deletion, and object to processing. Email [pieter@donche.org](mailto:pieter@donche.org). If you're not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).
